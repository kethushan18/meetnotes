Set up MeetNotes

About two minutes, once. Nothing to do after this.

    What should we call you?

    Your own voice is labelled with this name in transcripts and action items.

    Pin MeetNotes to your toolbar

    You'll click this icon on your meeting tab to record. Chrome only allows recording a tab after that click.

    1. Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome.
    2. Click the pin next to MeetNotes.
    Waiting for the pin...

    Shortcut you can also use on a meeting tab: Alt+Shift+M

    Allow your microphone

    So your own voice is in the transcript. The meeting tab gives everyone else. Audio never leaves your Mac.

    Download the speech engine

    One download of about 150 MB, kept in Chrome. It turns speech into text and tells voices apart, all on your Mac. You can continue while it downloads.

    Connect the Claude app

    After a meeting, "Open in Claude" starts a new chat in your Claude app with the transcript ready to send.

    If Chrome asks "Open Claude?", tick Always allow and click Open.

    You're all set

    1. Join your meeting in a Chrome tab (Sevv, Meet, Zoom web).
    2. Click the MeetNotes icon. The panel shows Ready.
    3. Add who's there (optional) and press Start recording.
    4. When a new voice speaks, tap their name once.
    5. Press Stop, then Open in Claude.

    Change anything later in Settings. API key, Google Drive and Slack options are under Advanced.
